--- Model Limitations ---
- Linearity Assumption: Linear regression assumes a strict linear relationship.
- Comparability: Industry/company observations may not be perfectly comparable.
- Scale & Outliers: Financial data can contain extreme scale differences.
- Causation: A strong correlation (R²) does not necessarily imply causation.
- Dataset Size: The model's predictive performance depends heavily on the quality of the industry dataset.
- Variance: Train/test split results may vary depending on the chosen random state.
